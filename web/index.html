Flood path (bonus)

Click Trace flood path, then click any point upstream on the map. The path follows the Copernicus DEM down the valley and lists settlements along it.

An inspector agent looks again at every part of the map that matters, most exposed first. It measures each 600 m cell against nearby unflagged ground (OpenCV: co-registration, colour, structure, texture, vegetation, radar), fetches a fresh later image where clouds or mixed evidence leave doubt, and confirms only what several independent lines agree on. Everything else comes to you.

    Every report is fingerprinted: one Merkle root over all its files, one over the road status of every settlement. Once anchored on a public ledger, nobody (including us) can change a number without it showing.
